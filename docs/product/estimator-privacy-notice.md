## What we collect and why

We collect the business or personal tax answers you enter, including any optional note. We use them to calculate and save an indicative estimate and show where professional review is needed. Using the calculator is voluntary. We need answers to the required questions to calculate your estimate. Leave optional fields blank if you prefer. Please leave out identity numbers, tax reference numbers, account numbers and copies of records.

An unfinished draft is stored in your browser tab so you can continue. When you submit, we save your answers and the calculation used for your result and PDF. Anyone who has the result link can open it while it is available. Share it only with people you want to see the estimate.

The calculator can produce an estimate without your name, email address or phone number. Where a separate follow-up form is available, we use the contact details you choose to provide to handle the request you make. Marketing requires a separate choice.

## How long we keep it

- An estimate without a linked enquiry is kept for 90 days after creation.
- An unsuccessful enquiry is kept for 12 months after closure.
- We keep successful client records according to our client-record policy and applicable legal obligations.
- An unfinished browser draft expires seven days after creation. Resetting or completing the estimate clears the draft, and the browser may clear it sooner.
- We may keep long-term aggregate statistics that cannot reasonably identify you.

We may keep specific records longer where legally permitted and necessary, for example to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute. We restrict how those records can be used while the exception applies.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct your information, object to certain processing, or request deletion or restriction where applicable. Use the Information Officer contact above. We may ask for enough information to verify your request. You can also lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator.
